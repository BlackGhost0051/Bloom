# Privacy Policy for Bloom

**Last updated:** August 29, 2026

## Overview

Bloom is a lightweight media management application that allows users to create custom folders and hide media files from the system media scanner using a `.nomedia` file. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user data.

## Data Collection

Bloom does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information of any kind.

## Permissions

Bloom requests the following permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Read media files on your device to display them in the app |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Create folders and `.nomedia` files on your device |
| `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Manage files across storage on Android 11+ |

These permissions are used exclusively on-device. No data is ever uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any server or third party.

## Third-Party Services

Bloom does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools.

## Data Storage

All files and folders managed by Bloom remain entirely on your device. The app does not use any remote storage, cloud services, or external databases.

## Children's Privacy

Bloom does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.
